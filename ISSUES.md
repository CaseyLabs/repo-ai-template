# Issue register

Authoritative register of unresolved issues, keyed by stable IDs shared with the [roadmap](PLAN.md). Each issue sits under its owning project's section; see [Add an issue](docs/PROJECT_MANAGEMENT.md#add-an-issue) for the required fields.

## Recurring problems

None. Track environment and tool symptoms here with their workaround and an occurrence count. On a second occurrence or a costly first one, move the workaround into the applicable guidance and remove the tally.
